What bargain film does

Bubbles inside a year. Fades purple in the sun. Peels off the edges and bakes you anyway, because dyed film blocks light, not heat. Then you pay twice: removal, then the real install.

What dealer film does

XPEL XR Black Ceramic rejects heat, cuts glare and UV, and comes through an authorized dealer, with the exact film and shade quoted before we touch the car. It goes on once, correctly.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is dark window tint legal in Illinois?
Illinois sets different limits for sedans than for SUVs and vans, and medical exemptions exist. Tell us your vehicle and the look you want; the quote comes back street legal, or flagged where it would not be.
What film do you install?
XPEL XR Black Ceramic for tint, installed as an authorized dealer. Light tint jobs use Luxe Film. The exact film and shade is agreed before any work starts.
Do you only do performance cars?
No. The feed shows Trackhawks and Supras next to a 2025 Kia K4. Daily drivers get the same film and the same install discipline.
How long does a tint job take?
Most full tint jobs are in and out the same day. Wraps and coatings get scheduled by scope when we lock your spec.
